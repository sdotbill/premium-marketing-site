You've closed dozens of deals — 41 in five years on the public record. But there's no system turning those closings into Google reviews. Local search runs on reviews. Every happy client who never leaves one is a ranking you handed to a competitor.

No active personal page posting your listings, your just-solds, your market updates. Every listing you close is content your competitors are posting — and you're not. In real estate, the agent with the loudest proof wins the listing appointment.
