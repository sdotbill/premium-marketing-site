What it is

Not a chatbot. An operator.

A chatbot answers questions. An operator has a job, a rank, a memory, and standing orders — and does the work between your decisions. The Pack is the complete structure our Dave runs on, packaged for yours:

📋

Role & rules architecture

Your operator’s job description, authority boundaries, and decide-and-act discipline as fill-in templates. You name your operator, set its rank, and draw its lines — including the ones it never crosses: signatures, money, and legal stay yours, hard-coded.

🧠

Memory system

The layered memory architecture — curated long-term memory, daily logs, people and business files — as an empty, documented structure your operator fills with your life and your business. It stops forgetting. So do you.

🔄

Decision discipline — the loop

Every meaningful output runs the loop: draft, critique, devil’s advocate, verdict. Decisions inside the operator’s authority ship, then get reported — you audit after, with veto. No approval-queue bottlenecks, no surprises.

⚡

Strike-learning

Every correction you make becomes a permanent standing rule. Your operator compounds — each mistake it makes once, it never makes twice. The rule base is the asset, and it grows every month you run it.

📈

Marketing module

The operator-run acquisition system: lead-pool discipline, campaign cadence governance, audit-first outreach, reply triage, and book/show follow-up management — the same module that gets our phones ringing.

🌉

Bridge protocol

Have a partner, a spouse, a GM? Their operator and yours coordinate peer-to-peer over ordinary email — proven live on October 17, 2026, when our two Daves started running this company together.
